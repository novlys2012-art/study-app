Audience: Slovak and Czech university students studying in daylight faculty libraries and one-handed on a 23:30 night tram before exams.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Alfa Skriptum turns Lekto into an authoritative Central European technical compendium (*Alfa Bratislava / SNTL Praha*), refusing weightless off-white-and-white SaaS card stacks and bookish cream-serif AI clichés alike.

OWN-WORLD: Deep Prussian-Cobalt cloth shell (`#10233d` light / `#090e16` dark) framing glare-free blueprint-tinted daylight sheets (`#eef3f8` / `#fbfdff`), Cobalt Press primary actions (`#154da3` / `#73b2ff`), crisp `4px`/`6px` binding corners, `Schibsted Grotesk` UI/tabular structure paired with optical-sized `Literata` solely in `#reader` prose, and unboxed Sulfur `<mark class="hl">` proof highlights (`#f4e547`) paired with outdented margin loci.

STORY: Under exam pressure, the student immediately sees what to study next, trusts every generated question because missed items jump straight to the Sulfur-marked sentence and page locus in their own uploaded file, and reads summaries comfortably for hours day or night.

FIRST VIEWPORT: `#welcome` opens with a full-bleed Prussian-Cloth masthead and hero stage pairing `Z prednášok rovno k skúške.` and the single primary CTA (`#wGuest`) on the left with a crisp daylight tear-proof sheet on the right showing a missed quiz question linked across a register rule to its Sulfur-highlighted `Literata` source passage and margin locus; `#today` leads with a Prussian-banded priority exam sheet and single Cobalt CTA (`Začať dnešné kolo`); `#reader` frames a calm `65ch` `Literata` column with Prussian scroll progress, locus metadata, sticky `Kľúčové pojmy` rail, and single Cobalt dock CTA.
